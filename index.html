PRODUCT RESEARCH · WORKFLOW TOOLS

Discover products. Review suppliers. Prepare marketing drafts.

M&A Discovery is a developing seller research tool. It helps users explore eBay product listings, review external supplier search links, record manual supplier comparisons, and prepare social media text drafts.

Research results are not proof of completed sales. Supplier matches, pricing, profitability, and product claims require independent verification.

What the app does today

  • Searches supported eBay marketplaces through official read-only APIs.
  • Opens external supplier searches and supports user-entered comparison notes.
  • Prepares TikTok and Instagram content drafts for review; it does not publish content.

Planned TikTok integration

TikTok Login Kit is planned for evaluation in a restricted development sandbox. TikTok account connection, video generation, publishing, and analytics are not currently available in the public-facing app. Any future integration will require user authorization and appropriate platform permissions.
